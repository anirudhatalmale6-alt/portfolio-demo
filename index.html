Identity, editorial and type design for people who care how it is made.

I design identities, books and typefaces. Twelve years of it, mostly for cultural institutions, independent brands and publishers who want the work to outlast the trend it was made in.

The process is unfashionably slow at the start and very fast at the end. I read the brief properly, draw a lot, throw most of it away, and hand over a system you can actually run without me. Available for commissions and collaborations.
